# SOLMORA original image prompts

Method: built-in image generation, with genuine transparent backgrounds for product cutouts. Selected outputs are copied into the project and served locally. No reference-brand photographs or artwork were copied.

## Product collection

Base prompt: “One premium photorealistic e-commerce cutout of a single 240g artisan Mediterranean preserve jar. Straight-on front view at label center, short broad cylindrical clear glass jar, rounded shoulders, thick refractive glass base, dense textured real fruit preserve fully filling it. Matte very dark burgundy metal twist-off lid with fine ridges. Ivory uncoated paper label wrapped realistically with curvature and shadows, high-contrast elegant burgundy serif typography, fine naturalistic botanical illustration matching the flavor, generous whitespace and thin border. Brand exact ‘solmora’, product text below, small ‘ORCHARD PRESERVE’ and ‘240 g’. Original luxury Mediterranean pantry brand. Soft studio light upper left, convincing glass reflections and material texture. Entire jar visible isolated on a genuine transparent background, no floor, no props, no watermark, no existing brands.”

Flavor variants:

- Golden orange: dense amber orange marmalade with fine peel shreds; orange branch illustration; ‘Golden orange’; category ‘ORCHARD MARMALADE’.
- Lemon & vanilla: pale golden lemon preserve, fine peel; lemon branch and vanilla flower illustration; ‘Lemon & vanilla’.
- Wild fig: deep purple preserve with small golden seeds; cut fig and leaf illustration; ‘Wild fig’.
- Blood orange: deep red-orange marmalade with peel shreds; blood orange segment and leaves; ‘Blood orange’; category ‘ORCHARD MARMALADE’.
- Apricot & rosemary: warm amber pulpy preserve; apricot and rosemary illustration; ‘Apricot & rosemary’.

The golden-orange prompt additionally emphasized realistic paper texture, glass embossing and no cast shadow. Generated text and fruit varieties were inspected before integration.
